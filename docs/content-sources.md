## Content handling

Published case study metrics and client feedback are reproduced from the owner's site, not independently audited. Preserve the source's qualifications: webinar registrations do not establish attendance or sales; reel results concern reach/follows; projects without measured performance describe delivered work only.

The source's animated counters and live slot-availability count are not copied as static numbers. Availability should be checked through the live application. Resource and course links retain their existing destinations. The archetype assessment remains marked as in development.

The existing Krafted Method and in-development ecosystem concepts remain accessible under The Method and Inside the Lab. These are distinct from the published client work.

Brand/project images and testimonial media use the owner's existing public CDN URLs. Their availability depends on those original URLs.
